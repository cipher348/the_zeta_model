## Gamma Phase

# A Quantile-Based Regression Framework for Physical Measurements with Calibrated Prediction Intervals

[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.5281/zenodo.21397331-blue)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21397331)

The methodological framework presents a fully documented, quantile-based regression framework for constructing calibrated, state
dependent prediction intervals from structured measurement data derived from the non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function. This
working paper focuses exclusively on methodological development, reproducibility, bootstrap validation, and deployment, while predictive
applications and theoretical interpretation are intentionally reserved for subsequent stages of the project.

## Beta Phase

# A Causal Drift Predictor for Relative Phase Dynamics

[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.5281/zenodo.22823985-blue)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22823985)

Beta Phase, Part I of The Zeta Model presents a causal drift predictor for relative phase dynamics within the Radiale Zahlenräume
framework. Using only information available up to each forecast anchor, an Extended Kalman Filter estimates the local phase and
frequency state and projects the relative phase forward. Across the dense anchor geometry, the predictor consistently outperforms a
frozen-phase baseline from the first tested horizon with a fully positive moving-block confidence interval onward, with the forecast
advantage increasing toward the longest tested horizon. The paper focuses on causal information boundaries, baseline comparison,
reproducibility, and transparent treatment of bootstrap limitations. Beta Part I establishes the dynamical predictor layer; Beta Part II
will address the transition toward a structural predictor of the zeta sequence.

# Conditioned Harmonic Geometry of Switch Ensembles

[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.5281/zenodo.23599324-blue)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23599324)

Several independent tests support a geometric interpretation at the ensemble level. Macro-scale switch organization is reproduced by
conditioned harmonic constructions without introducing an additional orbit-history term. In particular, adjacent-switch depletion and
redistribution toward larger jumps persist under independent detection and convention checks, while a previously apparent low-tier
boundary anomaly is resolved analytically.
The paper also reports negative predictive bridge tests. Simple local-state models do not establish a specific causal mapping from the
previously forecastable internal phase dynamics of Beta Phase Part I to future switch classes or switch-ensemble observables. A
subsequent sampling audit further shows that the fixed dynamical representation loses reliable per-zero resolution as its effective
samples-per-cycle approach the Nyquist limit. The conditional defect distribution remains structured at the ensemble level but broadens
and becomes asymmetric near the sampling boundary.
